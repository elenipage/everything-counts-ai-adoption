# Individual attitudes towards AI: exploratory analysis

A data analysis for the Everything Counts module of my MASc at The London Interdisciplinary School.

## The data

- **Source:** European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC), *CRONOS-3 Wave 5 edition 1.1*, published 2026.
- **Freely available from:** [ESS Data Portal](https://ess.sikt.no/en/datafile/c927b7cf-1355-4970-9c12-a8f53913f9d4)
- **Unit of observation:** one row = one person
- **Coverage:** Adults over the age of 18 within the following countries: Austria, Belgium, Czechia, Finland, France, United Kingdom, Hungary, Iceland, Poland, Portugal, Slovenia. 
- **Dates of data collection**: 14/05/2025 - 11/06/2025
- **Size:** 9545 × 26

## Data citation
European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC) (2026) CRONOS-3 Wave 5 edition 1.1 [Data set]. Sikt - Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research. https://doi.org/10.21338/cron3w5e01_1.
